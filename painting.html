Whispers of Autumn

24" x 36" • Oil on Canvas
$2,400 USD

Behind the Piece

This piece was born from a quiet morning walk through the tea gardens just outside the city. The way the fog clung to the leaves before burning off in the midday sun felt like a fleeting secret.

I wanted to capture not just the visual palette of the changing season, but the crisp, cool feeling of the air. The heavy palette knife strokes in the foreground are meant to ground the viewer, while the thinner, glazed layers in the background invite the eye to wander into the mist.
