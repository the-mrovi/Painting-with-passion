Hi, I’m Sanjana Rahman Nowrin.

Painting is something I truly love. It gives me a way to express feelings, ideas, and little moments that are sometimes difficult to put into words. I also love sharing my thoughts, experiences, and the things that inspire me.

This website is my little space where I share my artwork, thoughts, and highlights with you. You can explore my paintings, read my thoughts, and leave a like or comment if something connects with you.

If you love any of my artwork, you can place an order. And if you have something special in mind, feel free to share your idea with me. I’ll be happy to understand your request and try my best to create something meaningful for you, In Sha Allah.

You can also reach me on Instagram through Painting with Passion. Feel free to message me there for orders, custom requests, questions, or just to talk about my work.
