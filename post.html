Finding Light in the Shadows

By Sanjana Rahman Nowrin • June 18, 2026

The creative process is rarely linear. This past month in the studio has been an exploration of contrast—not just in the pigments I mix, but in the quiet moments between the brushstrokes. Embracing the darker tones has allowed the vibrant colors to truly breathe and take on a life of their own. Sometimes you have to dive deep into the shadows to understand the brilliant nature of light.

When I started this new series, I was hesitant. The blank canvas is always intimidating, but choosing to prime it with a deep, charcoal wash felt like a definitive commitment to the unknown. As the first layers of crimson and gold touched the surface, I realized that the dark background was not swallowing the color, but rather giving it a stage to perform.

[ Artwork Image Placeholder: "Midnight Serenade" ]
Midnight Serenade - Oil on Canvas

This interplay between the unseen and the seen has become a central theme in my work. It mirrors the human experience—how our quietest, most hidden moments often inform the brightest parts of our lives. I encourage you to look closely at the edges where the light meets the dark; that is where the real story is told.

Thank you for joining me on this journey. I look forward to sharing more updates as these pieces come closer to their final form. Until then, keep looking for the light.
